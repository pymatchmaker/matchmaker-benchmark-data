# URMP — attribution and licence

## Source

**URMP** is the University of Rochester Multimodal Performance dataset:

- Dataset: <https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.ng3r749>
- Publication: Li, Liu, Dinesh, Duan and Sharma, “Creating a multitrack classical music performance dataset for multimodal music analysis: Challenges, insights, and applications” (IEEE Transactions on Multimedia, 2018)

The dataset contains multitrack chamber and ensemble recordings together with scores and performance annotations for multimodal music analysis and alignment research.

## What is here

| Folder | Files | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `audio/` | 44 mp3 | URMP performance audio mixes |
| `score/` | 44 MIDI | Score files for the ensemble pieces |
| `annotations/` | 44 TSV | Performance-to-score alignment annotations |

The metadata index in `metadata-urmp.csv` is the authoritative list of the files in this folder.
