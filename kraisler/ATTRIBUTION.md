# KRAISLER — attribution and licence

## Source

**KRAISLER** is the multi-track piano-and-violin duet dataset released by Kim et al.:

- Dataset: <https://zenodo.org/records/21082251>
- Publication: Kim, Park, Lee, Kwon, Won and Nam, “KRAISLER: A Multi‑Track Dataset of Piano and Violin Duet Recordings for Music Information Retrieval Research” (TISMIR, 2026)

The folder contains the score and annotation files for a set of duet recordings together with multiple audio renderings and mixes for each excerpt.

## What is here

| Folder | Files | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `audio/` | 60 mp3 | KRAISLER audio mixes (dry, hall and studio variants) |
| `score/` | 20 MusicXML | Scores for the pieces in the corpus |
| `annotations/` | 20 TSV | Performance-to-score alignment annotations |

The metadata index in `metadata-kraisler.csv` is the authoritative list of the files in this folder.
